# Stock Market Dark Edit Guidance

## Transition notes
- Use hard cuts for panic and media scenes.
- Use slow dissolves for crash aftermath and reflection beats.
- Let the first 20 seconds breathe; do not over-cut the opening.
- Reset the eye after any dense mechanism section with either a portrait or negative space.

## Sound design cues
- low suspended bass for the opening and leverage section
- soft ticker and notification textures only in moderation
- dry room tone and phone vibration detail for human-cost scenes
- remove percussive motion almost entirely for the realistic-profitability section

## Pacing notes
- visual change every 2 to 5 seconds
- no more than two kinetic scenes back-to-back
- after each crash/history beat, return to a person or household consequence
- keep the ending slower than the middle

## Subtitle and text behavior
- short phrases only
- never put full-paragraph reading tasks on screen
- isolate numbers and year markers when needed
- use red sparingly so it still feels dangerous when it appears

## Budget notes
- final MP4 should be rendered from stills, motion design, and local audio
- Venice budget is allocated primarily to still-image generation, not video clips
- optional Venice video upgrades should be treated as post-v1 luxuries, not dependencies
