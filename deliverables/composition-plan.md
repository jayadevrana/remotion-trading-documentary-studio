# Remotion Composition Plan

## Composition
- `1920x1080`
- `60 fps`
- `4020` frames total
- One main composition: `TradingStrategyVideo`

## Production Approach
- Use a sanitized master clip exported from the supplied recording before the Remotion timeline.
- Build scene-based `Sequence` blocks so each section has its own zoom path, title card, and callout timing.
- Use a dark financial-tech background with a framed white chart canvas for a premium YouTube look.
- Keep the video itself muted and drive pacing from the generated narration.
- Burn in modern captions near the bottom for clarity and retention.

## Motion System
- Slow controlled zooms with light pan shifts.
- Soft glassmorphism title cards and stat chips.
- Gold highlight boxes on the chart and report areas.
- Fade-in and fade-out scene transitions rather than loud wipes.

## Privacy Handling
- Remove browser chrome and bottom UI noise in the preprocessed master clip.
- Keep the final composition focused on the TradingView canvas and visible report panels only.
