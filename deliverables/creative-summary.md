# Creative Summary

- The edit is built around chart readability first: clean crop, controlled zooms, and restrained callouts.
- The narration avoids exaggerated performance claims and only references metrics that are visible in the supplied report section.
- The pacing is intentionally condensed so the viewer sees the bullish sequence, bearish shift, mixed conditions, and report panel without dead time.
- The CTA is short, premium, and placed at the end over the trade list so it feels connected to the strategy showcase.
